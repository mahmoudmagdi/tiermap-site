Tiermap

Localize App Prices · PPP & Subscription Pricing

Set fair prices in every country on the App Store and Google Play at once: your own country tiers, PPP presets, and a preview of every change before anything is sent.

Charging $9.99 everywhere means you're too expensive in most of the world. Tiermap sets a fair price in every country — for every subscription and in-app purchase — in a few clicks.

Price by tiers, not by country

Group countries into your own tiers (or start from PPP – purchasing power parity – data) and set one price per tier.

Every way to change a price

One country, a selection of countries, a whole tier, or several products at once.

Safe by design

Every change is previewed first: increases, decreases, unusually large changes and the effect on existing subscribers are called out.

Both stores, one place

See your App Store and Google Play apps side by side, link the same plans across stores, and spot when their prices drift apart.

Private

Your API keys stay in your Mac's Keychain and Tiermap talks directly to the stores. No account, no server, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Try it first

Explore everything with sample data before connecting a store. Browsing, tiers, previews, exports and History are free; Tiermap Pro lets you send changes to the stores.
