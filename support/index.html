Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We're here to help.

Open an issue

The fastest way to get help is to open an issue on GitHub. Please include your macOS version, the Tiermap version (Tiermap → About Tiermap) and what you expected to happen. Never paste API keys or other secrets.

Connect App Store Connect

  1. Create a key in App Store Connect → Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API. It needs the App Manager or Admin role to change prices.
  2. Enter the Issuer ID and Key ID.
  3. Paste the contents of your AuthKey_XXXXXXXXXX.p8 file, or choose the file.
  4. Click Validate & Save. The key is stored only in your Keychain.

Connect Google Play

  1. In Google Cloud, enable the Google Play Android Developer API and the Google Play Developer Reporting API.
  2. Create a service account, then Keys → Add key → JSON, and download the file.
  3. In Play Console → Users and permissions, invite the service account's e-mail with access to your apps, View financial data and Manage store presence.
  4. In Tiermap, click Choose JSON Key…. The key is stored only in your Keychain.

Common questions

Can I try Tiermap without my keys?

Yes. Choose Try with Sample Data on the first screen (or Help → Explore Sample Data). Nothing is sent to App Store Connect or Google Play.

What does Tiermap Pro add?

Browsing, tiers, previews, exports and History are free. Tiermap Pro lets you send changes to the stores: monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore or cancel Tiermap Pro?

Use Restore Purchases in the paywall or in Settings → Tiermap Pro. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings; they renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

Where is my data?

API keys are in your Mac's Keychain; tiers, targets, links and History are in the app's container on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
